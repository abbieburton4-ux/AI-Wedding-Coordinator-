# Caterers: Wildman BT preferred list, grouped by published price

Research by the product lane, 2026-11-09. The 37 names are the venue's preferred caterer list, as the owner gave it ("these are the approved caterers for my wedding in Chicago", owner, 2026-11-09). Kosher catering is not required (owner, 2026-11-09).

**How to read the prices.** Every figure is a *starting* price that the caterer or a directory publishes, checked 2026-11-09. None is a quote. Unless a note says otherwise, the figure covers food only. Service charge (often 18–24%), tax, staff, rentals and the bar usually come on top ([eventplanning.com](https://eventplanning.com/caterers/chicago-il)). The venue is BYOB "as long as your caterer allows for it" ([Zola, Wildman BT](https://www.zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/wildman-bt)). An empty price means none was published and nothing is guessed. Where sources disagree, both figures are shown.

## Product-lane recommendation (proposed: the couple decides who to contact)

Ask about four caterers for quotes, one or two from each tier, so the couple can compare real numbers once the budget is set:
1. **Blue Plate**: has catered at Wildman BT and is a large full-service Chicago caterer. No price published.
2. **CMB Catering**: $108, and its 250-guest seated limit fits 225 invited.
3. **SBR / True Cuisine**: $60, a mid-price full-service option.
4. **Hearty Boys**: $42, says it works with realistic budgets. Lowest price for a full-service wedding caterer.
Optional fifth, if the budget allows premium: **Maison Cuisine** ($150).

Better as extras than as the main caterer for 225 guests: Grazing Table (cocktail hour), and Tamale Spaceship, Wooden Paddle or Chicago Food Truck Hub (late-night food). Ovy names under 150 guests as its best fit.

Open questions for the couple: catering budget ([GAP], waiting on the overall budget); service style (plated, family-style, buffet or stations); cuisine preferences; and whether the bar is part of catering (the venue is BYOB).
