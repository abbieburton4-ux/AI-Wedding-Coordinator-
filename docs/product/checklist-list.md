# Planning checklist: the starting list (PD-007)

The starting tasks for the planning checklist (backlog B24, PD-007). Part 1 describes the owner's list (brought by the owner on 2026-11-05). Part 2 is a **draft** list of Jewish traditions, written by the product lane for the couple to review. It is not part of the checklist until the couple approve it.

Each stage's due date is counted back from the wedding date. Until the couple fix the date, that's the provisional date in `wedding.md`.

## Part 1: the owner's list

19 stages, from "12 Months Before" to "Post-Wedding", about 100 tasks, each stage with its offset from the wedding date. The owner brought it on 2026-11-05 as `wedding-checklist-prompt.md`. It came from loverly.com (owner, 2026-11-05), so it isn't copied here: this project is public, and the list's wording belongs only in the app's own database (PD-007, call (e)). The engineer receives the list directly when the build is handed off.

Item ids are `<stage id>-<position>` (for example `12m-1`), and the order is the order given.

## Part 2: Jewish traditions (DRAFT, for the couple to review)

**Status: draft, not approved.** Written by the product lane on 2026-11-05 from general knowledge of common Jewish wedding customs, at the owner's request ("you can draft up an intiail jewish traditions list for me to review/add to"). Customs vary a lot by denomination and family. Several of these depend on your rabbi or officiant, so treat the list as things to ask about, not rules. Cross out what you don't want, and add what's missing. Nothing here goes into the app until you approve it.

| # | Stage | Task | Note |
| :- | :- | :- | :- |
| J1 | 12 Months Before | Check your date with your rabbi or officiant against Shabbat and the Jewish holidays | The provisional date, 21 Aug 2027, is a Saturday (see `wedding.md`) |
| J2 | 11 Months Before | Choose a rabbi or cantor, and ask what they require (for example a meeting before the wedding, or the witnesses' background) | Replaces the general "find the right person to officiate" item, if you want it to |
| J3 | 10 Months Before | Decide which ceremony traditions you're including (bedeken, circling, the seven blessings, breaking the glass, yichud) | Use this list as your starting point |
| J4 | 8 Months Before | Choose and order your ketubah (marriage contract): text, artist and wording | Custom ketubahs can take months |
| J5 | 8 Months Before | Decide on your chuppah (canopy): the venue's, rented, made by your florist, or a family tallit | |
| J6 | 6 Months Before | Decide whether the food will be kosher, and to what standard, and tell your caterer and venue | Affects the choice of caterer |
| J7 | 5 Months Before | Choose your ketubah witnesses | Some rabbis have requirements for who can be a witness |
| J8 | 5 Months Before | Check with your rabbi about the rings (some traditions use a plain band with no stones for the ceremony) | |
| J9 | 4 Months Before | Choose who will read or sing the seven blessings (sheva brachot) | |
| J10 | 4 Months Before | If the chuppah is held by hand, choose four people to hold the poles | |
| J11 | 3 Months Before | Order kippot (yarmulkes) for guests | |
| J12 | 3 Months Before | Write a short ceremony program that explains the traditions for guests who don't know them | |
| J13 | 2 Months Before | Tell your band or DJ about the hora, and ask the venue for sturdy chairs for the lift | |
| J14 | 2 Months Before | Plan the tisch and bedeken (veiling), if you're including them, and add them to the day's timeline | |
| J15 | 2 Months Before | Ask the venue for a private room for yichud (time alone together straight after the ceremony) | |
| J16 | 1 Month Before | Arrange an aufruf (being called up to the Torah) at your synagogue, usually on the Shabbat before the wedding | |
| J17 | 1 Month Before | Get the glass to break, and a pouch for it if you want to keep the pieces | |
| J18 | 1 Month Before | Get a kiddush cup and the wine for the blessings | |
| J19 | 2 Weeks Before | Visit the mikvah, if you want to | Optional, depends on your practice |
| J20 | 1 Week Before | Agree the processional order with your rabbi (in many Jewish weddings both sets of parents walk each of you down) | |
| J21 | The Day Before | Bring the ketubah, the glass, the kiddush cup and the wine to the venue | |
| J22 | Your Wedding Day! | Fast until the ceremony, if that's your custom | Optional |
| J23 | Your Wedding Day! | Sign the ketubah with your rabbi and witnesses before the ceremony | |
| J24 | Post-Wedding | Frame the ketubah, and turn the broken glass into a keepsake if you want one | |
| J25 | Post-Wedding | Plan sheva brachot meals in the week after, if you want them | Optional |
