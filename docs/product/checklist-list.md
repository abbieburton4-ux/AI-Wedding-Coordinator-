# Planning checklist: the starting list (PD-007)

The starting tasks for the planning checklist (backlog B24, PD-007). Part 1 describes the owner's list (brought by the owner on 2026-11-05). Part 2 is the Jewish traditions list the owner approved, with cuts.

Each stage's due date is counted back from the wedding date. Until the couple fix the date, that's the provisional date in `wedding.md`.

## Part 1: the owner's list

19 stages, from "12 Months Before" to "Post-Wedding", about 100 tasks, each stage with its offset from the wedding date. The owner brought it on 2026-11-05 as `wedding-checklist-prompt.md`. It came from loverly.com (owner, 2026-11-05), so it isn't copied here: this project is public, and the list's wording belongs only in the app's own database (PD-007, call (e)). The engineer receives the list directly when the build is handed off.

Item ids are `<stage id>-<position>` (for example `12m-1`), and the order is the order given.

## Part 2: Jewish traditions (approved by the owner, 2026-11-05)

**Status: approved, with cuts.** Owner (one of the couple), 2026-11-05: "cut J6, J14, J15, J16, J19, J22, J25 but keep the rest. combine find rabbi with find officiant". The rows marked *cut* are not added to the checklist. J2 replaces the owner's list's 11-months task "Research and find the right person to officiate the wedding" as one combined task: "Find your rabbi or officiant, and ask what they need from you (for example a meeting before the wedding, or rules about who can be a witness)". Originally drafted by the product lane from general knowledge of common Jewish wedding customs; customs vary, so check details with your rabbi.
